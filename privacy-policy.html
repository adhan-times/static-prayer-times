Privacy Policy – مواقيت الصلاة (Prayer Times – Al-Manar)

Last updated: September 28, 2026 (app version 2.0.0)

In short: the app does not ask for your name, email or account. It keeps an anonymous record so that prayer notifications reach your phone at your region's times, and it sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase so we can fix problems. Ads are shown through Google AdMob. We never sell your data.

باختصار: لا يطلب التطبيق اسمك أو بريدك الإلكتروني أو أي حساب. نحتفظ بسجل مجهول الهوية لإرسال تنبيهات الصلاة حسب مواقيت منطقتك، ونرسل إحصاءات استخدام مجهولة وتقارير أعطال إلى Google Firebase لإصلاح المشاكل. تُعرض الإعلانات عبر Google AdMob. لا نبيع بياناتك أبداً.

What we collect and why

DataWhyWhere it goes
Anonymous user record: a random Firebase user ID, the device's Android ID, device brand and model, Android version, app version, and a notification token To send prayer and reminder notifications to your device and to recognise the same device after a reinstall Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Messaging)
Notification settings: which prayers you want alerts for, reminder minutes, adhan sound on/off So the server sends the alerts you chose Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore)
Your selected region (e.g. Beirut, Tyre, Tripoli) To show and send prayer times for your region's calendar On your device, and in Firestore with your notification settings
Usage statistics: screens viewed, features used (for example opening a dua, changing region, turning an alert on), app and device information To understand which features are used and improve the app Google Firebase Analytics
Crash and error reports: technical logs, device model, Android version, app version To find and fix bugs Google Firebase Crashlytics
Advertising data: advertising ID, device information, IP address To show ads that keep the app free Google AdMob

Location

If you tap “تحديد منطقتي تلقائياً” (detect my region) or use the Qibla compass, the app reads your device location on the device only to pick the nearest region or calculate the Qibla direction. Your GPS coordinates are not sent to us or stored on any server. Only the region you end up choosing is stored, as described above.

Stored only on your device

Cached prayer times (for offline use), theme, font size, favourite duas and reading position. These never leave your device.

Permissions

  • Location (optional): region detection and Qibla direction, on the device only.
  • Notifications: prayer alerts and reminders; you choose which ones.
  • Internet: downloading prayer calendars, notifications, analytics, crash reports and ads.
  • Exact alarms, foreground service, wake lock, audio: delivering alerts on time and playing the adhan.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Messaging, Analytics, Crashlytics): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device's Google settings.
  • Prayer calendars are downloaded from a public file host (GitHub Pages) based on Al-Manar's published calendars; only the region name is part of the request. The Hijri date is fetched from AlAdhan (aladhan.com) without any personal data.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data and do not share it for marketing. Data is processed by the Google services listed above on our behalf. We may disclose information only if required by law.

Security and retention

  • All network traffic uses encrypted HTTPS.
  • Your anonymous record and settings are kept while the app is in use and removed on request.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days; analytics data follows Google Analytics' retention settings (up to 14 months).

Your choices

  • Deletion: email timesadhan@gmail.com and we will delete the data linked to your device. Uninstalling removes everything stored on the device.
  • Turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings at any time.
  • Deny or revoke the location permission at any time; you can pick your region manually.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
