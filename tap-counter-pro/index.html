# Tap Counter Pro

Tap Counter Pro

A simple, offline-first tally counter for Android. Count anything — laps, reps, tasbih, inventory, visitors — with goals, cycles, groups, statistics, daily reminders and a home-screen widget.

Last updated: 2026-07-28

Your counters live on your own device. There is no account to create, nothing to sign up for, and the app works completely offline. Signing in and backing up to the cloud is entirely optional.
